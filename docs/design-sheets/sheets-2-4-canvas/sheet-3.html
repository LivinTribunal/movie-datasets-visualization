Title:Film Lab Author:[NAME, UČO] Date:[DATE] Sheet:3 of 5 Task:T2 money vs ratings, T3 critic–audience gap, T5 budget vs popularity and ratings, X1 genre ROI

Operations

1. Pick an axis preset in F. Critics vs audience (T3), revenue vs critics (T2), budget vs rating with bubble size = votes (T5), ROI per genre (X1). The axes animate to the new positions, so you can follow the same films.

2. Brush in A. The brushed films fill the table D, and their gap distribution is drawn as an outline over every ridge in B.

3. Zoom and pan in A. Zoomed out, the points become hexbins so dense areas do not turn into one blob.

4. Hover a point or a table row. Card C shows all scores and the money with its source.

5. Click a ridge in B. Filters A and D to that genre family. Click again to clear.

6. Switch subset and USD in F. Notable films (≥ 10k votes) or all; nominal or 2025 USD. The axis labels change with it.

Focus

Big idea: one scatter, many questions. The same 25k films are re-plotted by axis presets, and every other view explains the selection you brushed.

Gap colour: PuOr, never red–green. Position already shows the gap; colour makes the outliers pop.
Money preset: log x axis that says so on the label; bubble = IMDb votes. Films without a budget are left out and counted in a note ("[N] films hidden: no budget").
Zoomed out: hexbin density (sequential Blues), so the overplotting of ~25k points does not hide the shape.

Discussion

Supports
  • T2, T3, T5 and X1 with position, the most precise channel.
  • Outliers can be found and named: the most interesting films for T3.
  • Brushing links "which films" (A, D) with "how typical" (B).
  • Money always shows where it came from and whether it is inflation-adjusted.
Misses
  • No countries, so T1 and X2 are not covered.
  • No time axis or series, so T4 and T6 are not covered.
  • Presets can confuse: the same dot moves when the axes change.
  • Many films lack a budget, so the money presets show a smaller, biased subset.
