Operations

1. Switch tabs. Years, genre, subset, USD and all selections stay. F is always on top, and G opens from any tab.

2. Atlas: pick a genre in B (or a chip in F). Click or search countries (up to 6): each adds a bar pair in C and a line in D.

3. Atlas: zoom and pan. Scroll or +/−, drag to pan, double-click a country to zoom to it.

4. Atlas: switch the map mode (share, top genre, cinema − streaming). Only A recolours. D keeps B's genre. In top-genre mode a legend swatch selects that genre.

5. Atlas: brush years in D. F follows, and A and C recompute for that window.

6. Film Lab: X ▾ and Y ▾ in A. Any film measure on each axis. The same field twice is greyed out. Money and votes go log by themselves.

7. Film Lab: brush in A. The table D fills. Sort by any column, hover a row to fill C and the drawer G.

8. Then and Again: measure ▾ in A, pick a franchise in B, X ▾ / Y ▾ in C. The area always shows a share of something countable.

9. Carry-over. Countries picked in Atlas filter Film Lab to films made there. A franchise picked in Then and Again is highlighted in Film Lab.

Focus

Big idea: three rooms, one memory. Each tab answers its own tasks, and the shared store carries your choices between them.

1. What carries over. One store feeds every tab. Filters travel everywhere, and a pick in one room becomes a filter or a highlight in the next.

Detail

Data. Films: Released, 1900–2026, ≥ 1,000 IMDb votes; money views use ≥ 10,000. TMDB + IMDb snapshot 2026-10-01; Netflix top 10 up to 2026-09-27; box office per country (Box Office Mojo); cinema admissions (LUMIERE); Rotten Tomatoes, Metacritic, Letterboxd scores; money from TMDB, The Numbers, Wikidata, Wikipedia; CPI + FX to 2025 USD. A Python (polars) pipeline writes static files for the app.
Shown as transformed. "2025 USD" label and a source badge on every money value; scores rescaled to 0–100; fuzzy-matched titles marked; thin data (< 30 entries) gets a dotted outline; "[N] films hidden: no budget".
Technology. React + TypeScript + Vite, D3 (scales, geo, brush, zoom), zustand store shared by all views, scatter on canvas (~25k points), the rest SVG. Static build, runs locally and on GitHub Pages.
Colours. 8 Okabe–Ito swatches, a Blues ramp, a PuOr ramp, a hatch swatch. Checked with a colour-blindness simulator.
