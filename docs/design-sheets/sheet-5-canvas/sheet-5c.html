Title:Movie Explorer Author:Štefan Moravík, Matúš Buzgo, Emma Tomášová Date:[DATE] Sheet:5 of 5 (final), variant C, core three Task:T1 genre taste per country, T2 money vs ratings, T3 critic–audience gap, T4 genre over time, T5 budget vs popularity, T6 sequels (scatter preset), X1 genre ROI, X2 cinema vs streaming

Operations

1. Pick a genre in B or click a legend swatch. One genre is selected for the whole app. The map mode only recolours A.

2. Click or search countries in A (up to 6). They filter C, D and E.

3. Zoom and pan A with scroll, +/− or a double-click on a country.

4. Brush years in C. The year range in F follows, and every view recomputes for that window.

5. Choose any X and Y in D. The same field on both axes is greyed out. Money and votes switch to a log axis on their own.

6. Use the presets as one-click shortcuts for common axis pairs.

7. Use "do sequels get worse?" It sets X to installment # and Y to rating relative to film #1. Every franchise is a thin grey line. Pick one in F or click it to colour and label it.

8. Brush in D to fill E. Sort E by any column. Hover a row or a point to fill G.

9. Hover a band in C. Other genres fade in D.

Focus

Big idea: few views, flexible axes. The scatter answers the film-level questions, sequels included.

One scatter, any pair. Left: critics vs audience (answers item 3, separate X ▾ and Y ▾, presets only as shortcuts). Right: the sequels preset sets both axes, every franchise is a grey line, one is coloured. With any other axes the picked franchise stays joined by a line, so it can be read as money vs rating too (item 7). The only blocked pair is the same field on both axes.

One selected genre. The map mode (share, top genre, cinema − streaming) only recolours A. B, C and the rest always show the selected genre (items 1, 2). The tooltip says both: "top genre: Drama · selected (Comedy): [XX] %".
Table and stacked area. E has in-cell bars and PuOr gap cells, sortable by any column (item 4, LineUp.js as the model). C shows a share of a countable measure, so "share of IMDb votes (%)" means popularity, not rating (item 6). Brush, then table, then card (item 5).

Detail

Data. Films (Released, 1900–2026, ≥ 1,000 IMDb votes; money views use ≥ 10,000). TMDB + IMDb snapshot 2026-10-01; Netflix top 10 up to 2026-09-27; box office per country (Box Office Mojo); cinema admissions (LUMIERE); Rotten Tomatoes, Metacritic, Letterboxd scores; money from TMDB, The Numbers, Wikidata, Wikipedia; CPI + FX to 2025 USD. A Python (polars) pipeline writes static files for the app.
Shown as transformed. "2025 USD" label and a source badge on every money value; scores rescaled to 0–100; fuzzy-matched titles marked; thin data (< 30 entries) gets a dotted outline; "[N] films hidden: no budget".
Technology. React + TypeScript + Vite, D3 (scales, geo, brush, zoom), zustand store shared by all views, scatter on canvas (~25k points), the rest SVG. Static build, runs locally and on GitHub Pages.
Colours. 8 Okabe–Ito swatches with short labels, Blues ramp, PuOr ramp, hatch swatch. Checked with a colour-blindness simulator.
