Where do critics and audiences disagree the most?

Data: tomatometer, audience score, gap = audience − critics, genre, year. data-to-viz paths: two numeric variables · one numeric value, several categories

AMAIN VIEW

Scatter plot with a diagonal

data-to-viz.com/graph/scatter
Encoding · x = critics, y = audience, a y = x reference line; colour = gap (diverging purple ↔ orange); the most extreme films labelled.
Good at · outliers are literally far from the line; "critic darlings" and "crowd pleasers" sit in opposite corners.
Weak at · overplotting in the middle; labels are hard, so label only the top N.
BLINKED · PER-GENRE SUMMARY

Ridgeline plot

data-to-viz.com/graph/ridgeline
Encoding · one density curve of the gap per genre, sorted by median gap; a reference line at 0.
Good at · compares the gap distribution across ~19 genres compactly ("Horror: audiences harsher").
Weak at · no film names; overlaps take care to read. D (violin) was the alternative.
CDROPPED

Lollipop / dumbbell

data-to-viz.com/graph/lollipop
Encoding · the top 20 films by absolute gap; two marks (critics ● and audience ■) joined by a line, sorted by gap.
Good at · names the outliers, the "identify" part of the task; easy to read.
Weak at · shows only the extremes; needs a filter (genre, decade) to stay interesting.
DDROPPED

Violin plot

data-to-viz.com/graph/violin
Encoding · one violin of the gap per genre, sorted by median gap; inner box = IQR, dot = median; a reference line at 0.
Good at · same per-genre comparison as B, with no overlaps and a shared baseline; median and spread are read directly.
Weak at · no film names; small genres give misleadingly smooth shapes, so show n per genre (caveat: boxplot hides n).
