How do ratings and earnings change across film series? Do sequels get worse?

Data: franchise → ordered installments, rating, real revenue, budget. data-to-viz path: ordered numeric per group

AMAIN VIEW

Line chart (highlighted spaghetti)

data-to-viz.com/graph/line
Encoding · x = installment number, y = rating (or revenue relative to film 1); all franchises light grey, selected ones highlighted, the median bold.
Good at · shows the general "sequel curve" and lets you pick out single franchises (spaghetti, solved by highlighting).
Weak at · busy without good highlighting; y must be relative (Δ vs film 1) to compare franchises fairly.
BLINKED · ONE-FRANCHISE DETAIL

Connected scatter plot

data-to-viz.com/graph/connectedscatter
Encoding · x = real revenue (log), y = rating; points of one franchise connected in installment order with arrows.
Good at · money and quality together: does a franchise lose both, or keep earning while ratings drop?
Weak at · readable for only 1–3 franchises at once; counter-intuitive for new users.
CDROPPED

Heatmap

data-to-viz.com/graph/heatmap
Encoding · rows = franchises (≥ 3 films), columns = installment 1…n, colour = change vs film 1 (diverging purple–orange).
Good at · browses hundreds of franchises at once; after sorting, the exceptions (series that improve) are easy to spot.
Weak at · no absolute values; long franchises make the grid sparse.
