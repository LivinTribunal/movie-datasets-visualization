T5Action: discoverTarget: correlation, compared across genres

Does a higher budget lead to greater popularity and better ratings?

Data: budget (USD 2025), IMDb votes (popularity), ratings, genre. data-to-viz path: three numeric variables plus a category

AMAIN VIEW

Bubble chart

data-to-viz.com/graph/bubble
Encoding · x = budget (log), y = rating, size = IMDb votes, colour = genre family.
Good at · puts all three measures in one view; blockbusters stand out.
Weak at · sizes are read poorly (radius vs area); thousands of bubbles overlap.
BADVANCED VIEW

Scatter plot, small multiples per genre

data-to-viz.com/graph/scatter
Encoding · one panel per genre family: x = budget (log), y = rating (switch to votes), each with a trend line.
Good at · directly answers "does it differ by genre?", since each panel's slope can be compared.
Weak at · many panels; small multiples need shared axes and enough films per genre.
CDROPPED

Heatmap

data-to-viz.com/graph/heatmap
Encoding · rows = genre families, columns = budget deciles, colour = median rating (switch: median votes).
Good at · a compact, aggregated answer; clear patterns like "rating flat but votes rise with budget".
Weak at · hides the spread inside each cell; the choice of bins changes the picture (caveat).
Team decision (10.10.2026): A is the main view. B is the advanced view (split by genre), like in T4. C is dropped.
