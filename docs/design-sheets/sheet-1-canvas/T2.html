Are commercially successful films also better rated?

Data: revenue or ROI (USD 2025) vs critic and audience scores, ~13k notable films. data-to-viz paths: two numeric variables · several numeric variables

AMAIN VIEW

Scatter plot

data-to-viz.com/graph/scatter
Encoding · x = worldwide gross (log), y = score; switch critics (tomatometer) / audience (RT audience, IMDb); trend line per score type.
Good at · shows the relationship and individual films; supports brushing, hover and selection for linked views.
Weak at · 13k points overplot, so it needs transparency, small dots or zoom.
BLINKED · ZOOMED-OUT MODE

2D density / hexbin

data-to-viz.com/graph/density2d
Encoding · same axes as A; colour = films per hexagon (sequential). Small multiples: critics next to audience.
Good at · solves overplotting and shows where most films sit; a fair side-by-side of the two score types.
Weak at · loses individual films (no hover on one film); harder to link to the other views.
CDROPPED

Correlogram

data-to-viz.com/graph/correlogram
Encoding · matrix of budget, revenue, ROI, tomatometer, metascore, audience score, IMDb, votes; cell = correlation (diverging purple–orange).
Good at · answers "critics, audiences, or both?" at a glance and compares all metrics at once.
Weak at · abstract: one number per pair hides non-linear patterns and outliers. Better as a summary next to A.
