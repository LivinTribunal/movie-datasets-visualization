How has the popularity of genres changed over time?

Data: year × genre family × (releases, IMDb votes, real box office, Netflix chart weight). data-to-viz path: time series, several groups

AFALLBACK FOR B

Streamgraph

data-to-viz.com/graph/streamgraph
Encoding · x = year, stream thickness = the genre's value, colour = genre family.
Good at · engaging and memorable; big shifts (the superhero / sci-fi boom) show at a glance.
Weak at · exact values and non-adjacent streams are hard to compare; the wiggle baseline distorts.
BMAIN VIEW · TIME BRUSH

Stacked area (100 %)

data-to-viz.com/graph/stackedarea
Encoding · x = year, y = share of the total, layers = genre families.
Good at · shows share (composition) honestly; a brush on x gives the global year filter.
Weak at · only the bottom layer has a common baseline, so the upper layers are hard to read.
CADVANCED VIEW

Line chart, small multiples

data-to-viz.com/graph/line
Encoding · one small panel per genre family, same x and y; the other genres in grey behind.
Good at · the most precise way to compare trends; avoids the spaghetti problem (caveat: small multiples).
Weak at · takes more space; less of an immediate overview.
Team decision (10.10.2026): B is the main view and holds the time brush. C is the advanced view. A stays as a fallback: if the 100 % shares confuse users about how big each genre really is, B is swapped for A.
