Which genres dominate cinemas and streaming in different countries?

Data: country × genre-family share from Netflix chart weight, box office and admissions. data-to-viz paths: map with a value per region · one value per category and subgroup

AMAIN VIEW · ENTRY

Choropleth map

data-to-viz.com/graph/choropleth
Encoding · country colour = share of the selected genre (sequential), or the top genre family (categorical). Toggle: Netflix / cinema.
Good at · the geographic overview; regional patterns (anime in East Asia) jump out; natural place to click a country.
Weak at · one genre at a time; big countries dominate, small ones vanish; no-data countries must be hatched.
BDROPPED

Heatmap

data-to-viz.com/graph/heatmap
Encoding · rows = countries ordered by similarity (clustered), columns = genre families, colour = share. Cinema and streaming panels side by side.
Good at · all genres for all countries at once; similar countries form visible blocks, which answers "how do countries differ" directly.
Weak at · 94 rows is tall, so it needs sorting or a region filter; colour is less precise than position.
CADVANCED VIEW

Stacked barplot (100 %)

data-to-viz.com/graph/barplot
Encoding · one bar per country, segments = genre families; a cinema bar and a streaming bar per country.
Good at · the exact genre mix for a few selected countries; a good drill-down after clicking the map.
Weak at · only the first segment shares a baseline; unreadable past ~15 countries. See caveat: order your data.
